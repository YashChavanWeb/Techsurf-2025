# Assets Management

- Contentstack provides a centralized **Assets repository** where you can store and manage various file types, including **images, videos, PDFs, and audio files**.

- This repository simplifies file management by enabling you to **reuse and organize your assets** across multiple entries in your stack.

---

## Features

- **File Upload and Storage:**
  Easily upload and securely store a variety of files into the Assets repository of your stack for future use.

- **Attachment to Entries:**
  Attach uploaded assets to entries seamlessly, allowing you to reuse the same asset across multiple entries to maintain consistency and save time.

- **Asset Management:**
  You can perform the following actions on assets:

  - **Upload:** Add new assets to your stack.
  - **Update:** Modify asset details as needed.
  - **Delete:** Remove assets from your stack.
  - **Publish:** Publish assets to specific environments and locales.
  - **Unpublish:** Unpublish assets from selected environments and locales as required.
  - **Filter:** Use filters to quickly find specific assets within your repository.

> **Note:** Assets added or updated in specific branches are **branch-specific**, with **URLs reflecting the branch name**, except for assets in the **main branch**.
